3. What is a Sprint in Scrum, and how does the Sprint Timeline help the development team? 

Description:
===========
A Sprint is a fixed time period (usually 1–4 weeks) in Scrum during which a team works to complete a set of User Stories from the backlog. It delivers a usable product increment at the end of each cycle.
A Sprint is a short, time-boxed iteration where development work is completed.
It helps teams focus on a defined set of tasks within a limited time.
Each Sprint ends with a review and improvement (retrospective).


->The Sprint Timeline shows the progress of work across the sprint duration (start → end). It helps teams track task status, identify delays, and ensure timely delivery.

Sprint timline:
===============

Sprint Timeline visually tracks progress of tasks day-by-day.
It helps identify delays, blockers, and workload distribution.
It ensures the team stays on schedule to complete sprint goals.

Additional Points:
==================
Sprint starts with Sprint Planning and ends with Review + Retrospective
Daily progress is monitored using Daily Stand-up meetings
Tools like JIRA provide Burndown Charts to visualize timeline progress
Helps improve team productivity and predictability
Ensures continuous delivery and feedback
