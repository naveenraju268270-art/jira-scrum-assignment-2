What are the key responsibilities of a Scrum Master in an Agile team?

Key Responsibilities of a Scrum Master in an Agile Team:
=======================================================

A Scrum Master in Scrum is responsible for ensuring that the team follows Agile principles, removes obstacles, and helps the team work efficiently to achieve sprint goals.

Description:
===========

A Scrum Master facilitates Agile practices and ensures smooth team workflow.
They remove blockers and support the development team.
They act as a bridge between the team and stakeholders.

Main Responsibilities:
=====================

Facilitates Scrum Events
Conducts Sprint Planning, Daily Stand-ups, Sprint Review, and Retrospective meetings.
Removes Impediments
Identifies and resolves issues that slow down the team’s progress.
Ensures Agile Practices
Makes sure the team follows Scrum rules and principles properly.
Supports the Team
Guides developers, encourages collaboration, and improves productivity.
Protects the Team
Shields the team from external distractions and unnecessary interruptions.
Improves Process
Continuously works on improving workflows and team performance.


Additional Description:
======================

Works closely with the Product Owner to manage the backlog
Helps in sprint planning and estimation
Tracks progress using tools like JIRA
Promotes communication, transparency, and teamwork
Ensures timely delivery of sprint goals
Acts as a coach and mentor rather than a manager
